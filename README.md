# PST-FoodLoop
🍱 FOODLOOP
Rescue food. Feed people. Reduce waste.
What is FoodLoop?
FoodLoop is a free surplus-food redistribution platform.
Restaurants, bakeries, hotels, canteens and events often have edible food left over. Instead of throwing it away, they can list it on FoodLoop for ₹0, allowing nearby people or verified organizations to claim it.
Surplus Food → FoodLoop → People / NGOs → Less Waste
Core Features
🏠 1. Home
Show nearby available food with:
Food name & photo
Quantity available
Distance
Pickup time
₹0 — Claim button
🍱 2. Food Details
Each listing should include:
Preparation time
Storage information
Veg / Non-veg
Allergens
Pickup deadline
Food-safety status
🗺️ 3. Map
Users can find available food nearby and filter by: distance, food type and urgency.
🤝 4. NGOs & Delivery Partners
Food can be collected or distributed through verified NGOs, social-service groups, college volunteers and authorized NCC/community groups.
🔐 5. Safety & Security
Provider safety confirmation
Expired food automatically removed
Users can report unsafe food
Verified delivery partners
QR-based handover tracking
Provider → Volunteer/NGO → Recipient
🤖 6. Smart Matching
Use AI to help predict surplus and match food with suitable nearby people or organizations based on quantity, distance, demand and pickup time.
♻️ 7. Impact
Track the difference FoodLoop is making:
Meals rescued
Food saved
People served
Waste prevented

FoodLoop is not another food-delivery or discount app.
